# AI Business Insights

> Generated by `src/ai_insights.py` from the analytics result files. Data is synthetic. Amounts in ₹ crore (1 Cr = 10 million).

**Top brand:** GlowCare (9.88% of revenue)

## 1. Revenue trend
**Finding:** Average monthly revenue over the last 6 months was ₹9.7 Cr, which grew 1.1% compared with the 6 months before.

**Recommended action:** Keep the campaigns and channels that supported this growth.

## 2. Discounts reduce profit
**Finding:** The 1-10% discount band earns a 34.0% margin, but the 30%+ band earns only 7.3%. The 30%+ band still sold 21,822 units.

**Recommended action:** Cap discounts at 20% for most products and review every deal above 30%.

## 3. Low-margin categories
**Finding:** Home & Kitchen, Fashion, Grocery, Footwear are below the company average margin; the lowest is Home & Kitchen at 22.76%.

**Recommended action:** Review supplier costs and prices for these categories first.

## 4. Customer segments
**Finding:** 2,096 customers are Champions, but 984 are At Risk and 2,236 are Lost (23% of segmented customers). The repeat purchase rate is 88.06% and the top 10% of customers bring 23.56% of revenue.

**Recommended action:** Send win-back offers to At Risk customers and reward Champions with early access.

## 5. Revenue anomalies
**Finding:** 2025-06 was unusually high (+8.6% vs baseline); 2026-02 was unusually low (-15.0% vs baseline).

**Recommended action:** Find out what happened in those months (campaigns, stock, delivery issues) and repeat or avoid it.

## 6. Payments and returns
**Finding:** About 16.7% of payments fail; Debit Card is highest at 17.04%. Return rates reach 9.18% for individual products (e.g. Premium Nova Product 732).

**Recommended action:** Investigate the payment failure rate and check quality or descriptions of the high-return products.
